Computational literary research

Stevenson Text Research Explorer

A browser-based companion to the reproducible Python and TEI/XML pipeline. Analytical choices and underlying tables remain visible rather than disappearing behind a visualization.

How to read this interface

Counts, distributions, networks, and statistical measures are evidence for further reading, not conclusions about literary meaning. Structural and entity decisions are stored in inspectable data or TEI markup.

Digital scholarly editing

TEI/XML layer

The project can now parse TEI divisions, encoded people/places/organizations, embedded document objects, correspondence metadata, explicit relation assertions, and document-circulation edges. The included XML file is explicitly a partial research encoding rather than a complete critical edition.

View the demonstration TEI/XML source

Research design

  • Structural divisions are stored as inspectable data or explicit markup.
  • Character aliases and TEI entity identifiers are version-controlled.
  • Frequency, dispersion, context, network, and comparative statistics are kept distinct.
  • Every displayed value remains available in a machine-readable table.
